Reflow

A Kindle-style reader for PDF books. It rebuilds paragraphs from the PDF, so you can change the font, size and margins. Tap the left or right side to turn pages. Tap the middle for controls.

Tip: you can also drop a PDF anywhere on this page. Your books stay on this device, in this browser. Nothing is uploaded. Best results: text-based PDFs (not scans) with one or two columns. Figures show as a link to the original page.
